01 / Scientific Inquiry

Research & Instrumentation

Developing accessible optical instruments, spectral classification models, and low-power embedded sensing systems for food authentication, healthcare diagnostics, environmental monitoring, and renewable energy.

Core Research Interests

  • Optical Instrumentation: Raman and fluorescence spectroscopy, multispectral imaging, and optical remote sensing platforms.
  • Machine Learning & Edge-AI: Multivariate analysis and embedded neural models for non-invasive spectroscopy and medical diagnostics.
  • Embedded Systems & Sensing: Distributed wireless sensor networks, autonomous telemetry nodes, and bio-aerosol detection.

Current Research Projects

  • 01 Machine learning approaches to commodity classification based on Raman and Fluorescence Spectroscopy
  • 02 Delay-tolerant ad hoc sensor network-based environment monitoring
  • 03 Remote sensing of bio-aerosols
